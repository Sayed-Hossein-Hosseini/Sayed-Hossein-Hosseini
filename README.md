## **📌 About**

I am a Computer Engineering graduate with an academic background in Artificial Intelligence from the University of Isfahan. My work and research interests focus on applying machine learning and intelligent systems to complex problems across computer science, medicine, finance, and the life sciences.

My interests span **computer vision and image analysis, medical image analysis, biomedical signal processing, multimodal AI, and Vision-Language Models (VLMs)**. I am also interested in AI applications in computational biology, drug discovery, and Alzheimer's disease research, as well as machine learning approaches to behavioral and personality analysis.

In parallel, I have pursued research in **Financial AI, Financial Natural Language Processing, and Financial Question Answering**. My undergraduate research journey includes a literature review in Financial AI and FinScope, a research framework focused on evaluating qualifier-to-value binding in financial question answering. This work reflects my broader interest in reliable AI evaluation and understanding whether models interpret information correctly, beyond simply generating plausible answers.

Across these areas, I value rigorous experimentation, thoughtful model design, clear documentation, and practical implementation. My goal is to bridge AI research and engineering by developing reliable, effective, and useful intelligent systems.

## 🤖 Selected Projects

My projects combine applied AI research with practical implementation across financial AI, biomedical applications, computer vision, natural language processing, and intelligent systems.

- [**Undergraduate Thesis — Financial AI**](https://github.com/Sayed-Hossein-Hosseini/Undergraduate_Thesis) — Undergraduate research on AI for financial market analysis, supported by two complementary repositories covering the main research and a related literature review.
- [**RhythmAttention — ECG Arrhythmia Classification**](https://github.com/Sayed-Hossein-Hosseini/RhythmAttention_Hybrid_CNN-Transformer_Architecture_for_Arrhythmia_Classification) — A hybrid CNN–Transformer architecture for ECG arrhythmia classification using the MIT-BIH dataset.
- [**GenkI-4K Smile Registration Suite**](https://github.com/Sayed-Hossein-Hosseini/GenkI-4K_Smile_Registration_Suite) — An end-to-end facial image analysis project involving face detection, dataset cleaning, smile-recognition fine-tuning, and image registration.
- [**Embedding-Driven NLP System**](https://github.com/Sayed-Hossein-Hosseini/Embedding-Driven_NLP_System_Sentiment_Analysis_News_Classification_using_Word2Vec_and_GloVe) — Text classification using Word2Vec and GloVe embeddings with Logistic Regression, k-nearest neighbors, and SVM.
- [**Node Classification in the Amazon Product Graph**](https://github.com/Sayed-Hossein-Hosseini/Node_Classification_in_the_Amazon_Product_Graph) — Graph-based product classification using relationships between products and customer purchasing patterns.
- [**Genetic Algorithm for Optimized Customer Classification**](https://github.com/Sayed-Hossein-Hosseini/Genetic_Algorithm_for_Optimized_Customer_Classification) — A machine learning project exploring genetic algorithms for feature selection and customer classification.
- [**Traffic Object Detection Dataset**](https://github.com/AITC-trafic-density/Dataset) — A dataset resource for training and evaluating object detection models in traffic-related scenarios.

## 🔒 Selected Private Research & Projects

Some of my research and development projects are maintained in private repositories while research outputs are being developed or prepared for publication.

- **FinScope** *(Private)* — A research framework for evaluating qualifier-to-value binding in financial question answering, focusing on cases where a model identifies a financial value but associates it with the wrong contextual qualifier.
